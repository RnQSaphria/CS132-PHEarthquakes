Motivation

The Philippines is a country that is in the Pacific Ring of Fire. This leads to us having to experience more calamities than the average country. One of the disasters that our country goes through a lot are earthquakes. Infrastructures in the Philippines are not made to handle the strength of earthquakes. Our group aims to know the locations that experience a lot of earthquakes and how we and our communities prepare for such calamities.


Study Overview

In our project, we will analyse seismic data of the Philippines in the hopes of building safer communities. This study will tackle these earthquakes that happen under the Philippines and try to look for patterns to help communities stand in much safer ground.

Hypothesis Testing

To test the hypothesis, we can see that the predictor variable is the Region (Categorical). The outcome variable here would be depth and magnitude which are both (Quantitative) Since there are two, we use the ANOVA Test


We first checked if our data is normal using Skew, Kurtosis, and Shapiro-Wilk

Magnitude:

  • Statistic: 0.9334
  • P-Value: 2.5732e-81
  • Skewness: 1.1028 (right-skewed)
  • Kurtosis: 1.7245 (heavy-tailed)
  • Result: NOT Normally Distributed

Depth:

  • Statistic: 0.5996
  • P-value: 1.0151e-127
  • Skewness: 5.2020 (right-skewed)
  • Kurtosis: 54.7106 (heavy-tailed)
  • Result: NOT Normally Distributed

Since the data is not normally distributed, we'll use log. From log, we get the following results:

Log-Transformed Magnitude:

  • Shapiro-Wilk P-Value: 2.1656e-42
  • Skewness: 0.2888
  • Kurtosis: -0.1628
  • Result: Normalized

Log-Transformed Depth:

  • Shapiro-Wilk P-Value: 7.9129e-75
  • Skewness: -0.5784
  • Kurtosis: 0.2794
  • Result: Normalized

Above, Graphed.


Fig 6. Description

Since the data is now finally normalized, we can then use one-way ANOVA for magnitude. From the results, we can retrieve:


One-Way ANOVA:

  • Test Statistic: 451.0068
  • P-Value: 0.0000e+00
  • Significance level: α = 0.05
  • Result: Significant Difference EXISTS amongst regions.

Since these are the results, we can determine that Magnitude between regions are different. We then are able to reject H1.



Result Analysis

From the start of our research, we have uncovered many findings regarding earthquakes in the Philippines. We got data from PHIVOLCS and used their dataset to determine the average magnitude and frequency of earthquakes throughout the regions in the Philippines. We were able to answer our hypothesis that earthquake magnitudes are different in all regions of the country.

In our EDA, we managed to answer the research questions posed by our group regarding this topic. And we can see that certain parts of the Philippines really are getting hit by more earthquakes, and some have more magnitude than other areas.

Based on the results, we can say that the Top 10 areas that have the highest average magnitude and frequency of earthquakes should be more prepared. Their local authorities should practice preventive measures and check structures in more earthquake-prone areas. This would lead to more lives being saved in the case of earthquakes happening in those regions.
